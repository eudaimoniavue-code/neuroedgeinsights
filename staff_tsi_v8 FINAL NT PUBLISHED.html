Intervention Playbook
Tier 1 — Watch
TSI 75–85
  • Universal Tier 1: Wellbeing Wednesday check-ins
  • HOD coffee chats (fortnightly)
  • Duty swap availability flagged
  • Monitor next pulse survey
Tier 2 — Amber
TSI 65–75
  • HOD 1:1 within 2 weeks
  • Workload swap arranged via HOD
  • AST mentor matched within 2 weeks
  • EAP info shared proactively
Tier 3 — Red
<TSI 65
  • EAP referral (formal)
  • Principal 1:1 within 5 working days
  • AST workload audit initiated
  • Fortnightly review cycle
Secondary Guardrail: Voluntary attrition rate ≤2.5% (baseline met) — only monitored when TSI ≥75 school-wide. TSI is the normalised composite of 4–5 indicators above, target ≥80/100 school-wide.
