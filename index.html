About NeuroHR Insights

We Believe Every Workforce
Crisis Has a Neurological Cause

Founded to solve the retention crisis in education — and designed to scale across every high-attrition sector — we are the UK's specialist at the intersection of neuroscience, psychology, and HR data.

Mission & Vision

Why We Exist

Traditional HR metrics — engagement scores, exit interviews, headcount ratios — describe what happened. Neuroscience tells us why it happened and, critically, what will happen next.

Our mission is to give HR leaders in high-attrition sectors the psychological intelligence to intervene early, build resilient cultures, and retain the professionals society depends on most.

We start with education because the crisis is acute, the data is compelling, and the human cost is immeasurable. Healthcare is next — where the same principles apply with even greater urgency.
